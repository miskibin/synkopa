Mix and route

Send a sound to a mixer insert Balance levels Add an effect Sidechain a compressor

Send a sound to a mixer insert

Route sounds to separate inserts to adjust their levels and effects independently.

  • Audio on a Playlist track → click the track’s mixer routing control or choose Mixer channel from its menu, then pick an insert.
  • Instrument in the Channel Rack → use the mixer channel control on the left of its row. Click to choose an insert. When the channel is following the clip track, double-click the control to give that channel a free insert of its own.

Open the Mixer with F9. The insert’s meter moves when its sound plays.

Balance levels

  1. Play a busy section in SONG mode.
  2. Lower sounds that overpower the rest. Pan sounds left or right. Use mute and solo to check which sounds each insert carries.
  3. Watch the Master meter. If it clips, reduce the levels feeding it, then listen again.

Add an effect

  1. Click the insert you want to process. Choose Add effect… or press Shift + F9.
  2. Pick an effect, such as Reverb on a piano insert.
  3. Play the song while you adjust the effect. Toggle the effect’s bypass to compare it with the original sound. Drag effect slots up or down to change their processing order.

Pick an effect by what you hear.

Sidechain a compressor

A sidechain uses one sound to control an effect on another. For a kick and bass, the kick triggers a dip in the bass on each hit.

  1. Route kick and bass to separate inserts.
  2. Right-click the kick insert and choose Sidechain to → the bass insert. Synkopa sets the kick as the source on an existing Clean Compressor or adds one.
  3. Play both sounds. On the bass Compressor, adjust Thresh so the red GR meter moves on kick hits. Shorten Release if the bass stays down too long.

To choose a source from the Compressor window, open Own input and select the kick insert.
