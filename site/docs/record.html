Record audio

Set up an input, record a take, and combine the best parts of multiple passes.

Set up your inputRecord a takeCombine takes from a loop

Set up your input

  1. Connect your microphone or interface. Go to File → Settings… → Audio and choose your Input and Output.
  2. Right-click the round Record button and enable Audio. Click the button to arm recording. The IN meter should move when you sing or play.
  3. For a multi-input interface, select the physical socket under Input channel in Audio settings. Input channels appear once recording or monitoring opens the device.

To hear the input through Synkopa, use View → Monitor input and listen on headphones to avoid feedback. If your interface already monitors directly, leave software monitoring off to avoid hearing both paths.

Record a take

  1. In the Playlist, open the destination track’s menu and choose Record into this track. If you leave the destination unassigned, Synkopa chooses the first free track.
  2. Click the Playlist ruler where the take should start. Leave the transport in SONG mode. Enable 1 BAR if you want a count-in.
  3. With Record armed, press Space, perform, and press Space again to stop. Your take appears in the Playlist. Disarm Record when you are ready to listen back.

If no clip appears, check the Audio record filter, the IN meter, and the destination label under the main menus. See recording troubleshooting if the meter does not move.

Combine takes from a loop

  1. Drag across the Playlist ruler to select the section to repeat. Arm Record and play through several passes.
  2. Stop. Double-click the resulting comp clip, or use its track header’s take control, to show the takes.
  3. Click a take name to use the whole take, or drag across a section of a take lane to use that part in the comp. Play back the joins in context.

If you only want to record one pass, clear the loop by double-clicking the ruler before starting.
