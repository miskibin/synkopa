Synkopa user guide

Synkopa is a free music-making app that works the FL Studio way: you build patterns in the Channel Rack, arrange them on the Playlist and mix them on the Mixer. This guide takes you from the first launch to an exported song.

  1. Install and set upInstall Synkopa and make sure you hear sound. 5 minutes.
  2. How Synkopa worksChannels, patterns, the Playlist and the Mixer, and which window does what. 5 minutes.
  3. Make your first songDrums, a melody, a short arrangement and an exported file. 10 minutes.
