Make your first song

In about ten minutes you will program a drum beat, add a short synth melody, turn the loop into a few bars of song and export it as an audio file. You need nothing but Synkopa and headphones or speakers.

1. Check your sound 2. Program drums 3. Add a melody 4. Make it a song 5. Save and export

1. Check that you hear sound

Before making anything, make sure Synkopa plays through your speakers. The demo song is the quickest test.

  1. Download Synkopa and install it. On a Mac, read Install and set up first: macOS blocks the first launch.
  2. Start Synkopa and choose File → Open demo project.
  3. Press Space.You hear a finished trap song. Press Space again to stop.

Silence? Pick your speakers or headphones in File → Settings… → Audio. Choose audio devices explains each setting.

New to Synkopa's windows? How Synkopa works explains channels, patterns, the Playlist and the Mixer in five minutes. This tutorial works without it, too.

2. Program one bar of drums

Drums are written in the Channel Rack. Each row is one sound (a channel), and its 16 buttons are the 16 sixteenth notes of one bar. A lit button plays the sound at that moment.

  1. Choose File → New project.The Channel Rack shows four drum sounds: Kick, Clap, Hat and Snare. If it is hidden, press F6.
  2. On the Kick row, click steps 1, 5, 9 and 13: one kick on every beat.
  3. On the Clap row, click steps 5 and 13: a clap on beats 2 and 4.
  4. On the Hat row, click every odd step (1, 3, 5 … 15).
  5. Make sure PAT is lit in the toolbar, then press Space.The bar loops. Keep it playing while you change steps.

Clicked a wrong step? Right-click it to clear it.

3. Add a melody

Melodies need pitch, so they are written in the Piano roll: keys run from bottom to top, time runs from left to right. First add an instrument to play them.

  1. Below the channels in the Channel Rack, click + Channel and choose Empty synth.A new row named Synth appears under the drums.
  2. Right-click the name Synth and choose Piano roll.
  3. Click in the grid to place a note. Place two or three notes within the first bar.
  4. To lengthen a note, drag its right edge. To change its pitch, drag it up or down.
  5. Press Space.The melody plays together with the drums, because both are in the same pattern.

Want a better sound than an empty synth? The + Channel menu also has ready-made synth sounds sorted by type (Bass, Lead, Pad…) and sampled pianos, strings and guitars. See Play instruments, or play the melody in from a MIDI keyboard.

4. Turn the loop into a song

So far you have one pattern: one bar of drums and melody. A song is made by placing patterns on the Playlist, the timeline in the middle of the window. Each placed copy is a clip.

  1. Stop playback and press F5 to bring the Playlist to the front.
  2. In the Browser on the left, open the PATTERNS tab. Press Alt + F8 if the Browser is hidden.
  3. Drag Pattern 1 onto Track 1 at bar 1.
  4. Drag it again to bar 2.Two clips of the same pattern sit side by side.
  5. Click the Playlist's ruler at bar 1.The toolbar switches to SONG: Space now plays the Playlist instead of the pattern.
  6. Press Space to hear both bars.

Both clips play the same pattern, so a change in the Channel Rack changes both. To change only one clip, right-click the bar with its name and choose Make unique.

For a real song, press F4 to start a second pattern for a variation, then mark the sections. See Arrange a song.

5. Save the project and export audio

Saving keeps your editable project. Exporting renders the song into an audio file anyone can play.

  1. Press Ctrl + S and choose a name.Synkopa saves a .synkopa project file.
  2. Choose File → Export audio….
  3. Set Output to Mix (one file with everything) and Range to Song (the whole Playlist).
  4. Set Format to WAV for full quality, or MP3 to share it.
  5. Click Export… and choose where to save the file.When the progress bar finishes, open the file in any audio player.

That is the whole loop: channels → pattern → Playlist → export. Next, write longer patterns, record vocals or mix the song.
