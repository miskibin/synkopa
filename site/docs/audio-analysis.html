Analyze and extract from audio

Got a sample, a demo or a finished song and want to build on it? Synkopa can tell you its tempo and key, write its chords into a pattern, turn a sung melody into notes, and split a mixed song into drums, bass, vocals and the rest. Every result is a starting point: check it by ear before you build on it.

Find the tempo and key

Matching your project's tempo to a sample keeps it on the grid, and knowing its key tells you which notes fit over it.

  1. Right-click the audio clip's name and choose Detect tempo, key and chords….The dialog lists tempo candidates, key candidates and a strip of chords.
  2. Play the clip and count along. Pick the BPM that matches the pulse you feel. Detectors often suggest half or double the real tempo, so the dialog shows those too.
  3. Click Set tempo.The project takes that tempo, and the clip's length is adjusted so it still plays the same audio.
  4. Check the key candidates against a held bass note or chord in the recording.

To estimate the tempo and key of everything in the project instead, right-click the BPM display in the toolbar and choose Detect tempo and key from the project….

  • No tempo found: the clip is too short or too quiet. Analyze a longer, clearer passage.
  • No key found: drums alone have no clear key. Use a part with chords or bass.
  • Result disagrees with your ears: trust your ears and leave the settings as they are.

If a vocal insert has Pitch Correct, the dialog can pass it the detected key with one click.

Turn chords into a pattern

Writing a recording's chords into a pattern lets you play them on your own instrument, or write a bass line and melody that fit.

  1. In the same dialog, click Download model if asked. Chord detection uses the ChordMini model, downloaded once.
  2. Choose Simple for plain major and minor chords, or Detailed to include sevenths, suspended and diminished chords.
  3. Click a chord, or drag across the strip to pick a range. Whole clip picks everything again.
  4. Choose the instrument that should play the chords: an existing channel or New piano channel.
  5. Click Send to piano roll.A new pattern with the chords is placed on the Playlist under the clip.
  6. Play both together and fix any wrong chord or octave in the Piano roll.

Turn a sung melody into notes

Converting a melody to MIDI notes lets an instrument double or replace the voice, or gives you the melody to edit in the Piano roll. It works best on one clear line, such as a solo vocal or a whistled idea.

  1. Right-click the clip's name and choose Convert to MIDI….A preview shows the sung pitch and the notes that will be written.
  2. Raise SHORTEST to drop very short notes, such as breaths and consonants.
  3. Narrow LOWEST and HIGHEST to drop notes the detector put an octave too high or low.
  4. Under Timing, keep As sung, or pick a grid to snap the notes to it.
  5. Choose the channel that plays the notes: a new piano or one of yours.
  6. Click Convert.A new pattern appears under the audio clip and opens in the Piano roll.
  7. Play them together and fix wrong starts, lengths or missing notes.
  • Preview is empty: widen the note range, lower SHORTEST or pick another part of the clip.
  • Two notes sung on the same pitch become one, or notes end too late: Better notes… points you to GAME, an optional note finder you download in the pitch editor.
  • Mixed song, not a solo voice: extract the vocal stem first and convert that.

Split a song into stems

Stems are the separate parts of a mixed recording: drums, bass, vocals and everything else. Splitting a song lets you remix it, practice over it without the vocal, or sample just the drums.

  1. Save the project.
  2. Right-click the clip's name and choose Extract stems….
  3. Tick the parts you need: Drums, Bass, Other (keys, guitars, pads and the rest) and Vocals.
  4. Click Extract. The first time, the button reads Download … and extract and also downloads the HTDemucs separation model once.A progress bar shows each stage. Separation takes a while; the dialog shows an estimate.
  5. To keep working meanwhile, click Run in background. The progress stays in the corner; click it to come back. Don't edit the clip until it finishes.
  6. When it is done, solo each stem and listen for bits of other instruments and watery artifacts.Each stem sits on a new track below the original, starting at the same beat. The original clip is muted.

One Ctrl + Z removes all the stems. Only the part of the clip that plays is separated, with its stretch, pitch, fades and gain; pitch-editor edits are not carried over. Clips longer than 30 minutes must be split first.

Want your own song's mixer tracks as separate files instead? That is exporting stems, not extracting them.

When a download or analysis fails

Chord detection, stem separation and the optional note finder each need a model downloaded once. After that, they run on your computer without internet.

  • Download failed: the dialog explains why. Reconnect and click Try again.
  • Audio file missing: relink the file (clip menu → Relink missing file…), then try again.
  • Clip changed during separation: restore the clip and start again.
