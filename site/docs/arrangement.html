Arrange a song

Place and vary clips Loop and mark sections Tempo and song key Notebook and lyrics

Place two patterns and make a variation

Start with the one-bar drum pattern and a separate synth or melody pattern. Press F5 to bring the Playlist forward. Open Browser → Patterns.

  1. In the Playlist, set Snap to bar for arranging. Drag the drum pattern from the Browser onto a lane at bar 1. Hold Shift and drag the placed clip to bar 2 to copy it.
  2. Place the melody on another lane, starting at bar 2. Switch to SONG and press Space. The melody enters after the first bar of drums. In PAT, playback is limited to the current pattern.
  3. Open the second drum clip’s context menu, choose Make unique, then double-click that clip. Change the last few hats in the copy. Play from bar 1 and check that the original still sounds the same.
  4. Use Trim when dragging a clip edge to shorten the audible portion. Use the Playlist’s Stretch mode on audio when the goal is to fit its duration to the grid.

If both instances change, undo the note edit and use Make unique first. If the clip lands early, switch to bar snap and drag it into place. Check track mute and SONG if a clip is silent.

Loop and mark sections

  1. At bar 1, right-click the ruler and choose Add marker here. Name it Intro. At bar 3, add Verse. You can also use Alt+T at the current song position.
  2. Click a marker to jump there. Drag it to correct the section boundary. Right-click it to rename, delete or choose Loop this section. Double-clicking the marker also loops up to the next marker.
  3. For a custom loop, drag on the ruler across the span you want. Switch to SONG and play to check the repeat. Double-click the ruler to clear the range and click a bar to reset your playback start.

Set tempo, meter and song key

Drag or scroll on the BPM value. Right-click it for preset values, half and double tempo, or use TAP on each beat of the groove. Turn on the metronome and play a few bars to verify the speed. The Playlist uses a fixed 4/4 grid. For a phrase that groups in threes, place accents by ear over that grid. A MIDI import can report time signatures other than 4/4 as unsupported.

Click No key or the current key beside the BPM, then choose the root and scale. For a C-minor melody choose C and Minor. The Piano roll now highlights that scale for writing. Existing notes keep their pitches. To remove the guide, reopen the picker and clear the key.

Write a tempo change or ramp with the CLI

The desktop BPM control sets a single tempo. To edit tempo points, save the project, close it in the app, and use the installed Synkopa CLI on a copy. This map starts at 110 BPM and ramps from bar 5 to bar 9, where it holds at 140 BPM. The positions are zero-based beats, four to a bar.

synkopa -p song.synkopa apply --json '[{"op":"setTempoPoints","points":[{"beat":0,"bpm":110},{"beat":16,"bpm":110,"ramp":true},{"beat":32,"bpm":140}]}]' --label "Raise tempo into chorus"

Append --dry-run first to preview the change. Set "ramp":false on the point at beat 16 for a jump at beat 32. Reopen the edited file and play or render across those bars to verify. To clear the map, apply [{"op":"setTempoPoints","points":[]}] or set the global BPM in the app. Adjusting the global BPM clears existing tempo points.

Line up lyrics in the Notebook

  1. Press F8, add a page, and double-click its tab to rename it Verse. Enter each bar of lyrics on its own line. An empty line takes one bar. A heading such as [Verse] takes zero bars.
  2. Use + to add a Chorus page and set from bar to the Chorus marker’s bar. Click a number beside the lyrics to jump straight to that bar.
  3. Start SONG playback. The current page’s tab lights up. Select that page to see the current bar highlighted and the text scroll to follow it. Return the playhead to the marker and check that the first lyric line lands with the intended beat.

If the text follows one bar late, check from bar and count empty lines as rests. Save with Ctrl+S to keep lyrics with the project. Ctrl+Z restores a deleted page. When reopening the same project, Synkopa can restore local unsaved lyrics if the saved Notebook has not changed since the backup.

Continue to record audio, mix and route, or export the song.
