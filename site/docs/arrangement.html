Arrange a song

You have a few patterns that loop. This page turns them into a song with a beginning, sections and an end: place them on the Playlist, vary the repeats, mark the sections, set the tempo and key, and line up the lyrics.

Place and vary clips Mark and loop sections Tempo and song key Lyrics in the Notebook

Place your patterns and make a variation

Click the four-corner Fit whole song icon beside the overview scrollbar (or press Shift+4) to show the full arrangement in time without changing track heights. The wider right scrollbar scrolls vertically; drag either end to zoom the lanes.

The Playlist is the song's timeline: bars run left to right, tracks top to bottom. Each pattern you place there is a clip. You need at least one pattern, for example the one-bar drum beat, and ideally a second one with a melody.

  1. Press F5 to bring the Playlist to the front, and open the Browser's PATTERNS tab.
  2. Set the Playlist's Snap to bar, so clips land exactly on bar lines.
  3. Drag the drum pattern onto Track 1 at bar 1.
  4. Hold Shift and drag that clip to bar 2.A copy of the clip sits at bar 2.
  5. Drag the melody pattern onto Track 2 at bar 2.
  6. Switch the toolbar to SONG and press Space.One bar of drums, then the melody joins in.

In PAT, Space only loops the current pattern. Arranging always needs SONG.

Change one repeat without changing the others

Every clip of a pattern plays the same notes. That is useful (fix a note once, every chorus follows), but a fill should change only the last repeat. Make unique gives one clip its own copy of the pattern.

  1. Right-click the name bar of the second drum clip and choose Make unique.
  2. Double-click that clip.Its new pattern opens in the Channel Rack.
  3. Change the last few hat steps.
  4. Play from bar 1.Bar 1 sounds as before; only bar 2 has the new hats.

Both clips changed? Undo the step edit, then do Make unique first.

Mark the sections and loop one of them

Markers are named labels above the ruler (Intro, Verse, Chorus). They let you jump around a long song and loop one section while you work on it.

  1. Right-click the Playlist ruler at bar 1 and choose Add marker here.
  2. Type Intro and press Enter.
  3. Add a Verse marker at bar 3 the same way. (Or press Alt + T to add a marker at the current song position.)
  4. Double-click the Intro marker.A loop covers bars 1–2, up to the next marker. Play in SONG to hear it repeat.

Set the tempo and the song key

The tempo (BPM, beats per minute) sets the speed of everything. The song key is the scale your notes belong to; setting it highlights that scale in every Piano roll, so wrong notes are easy to avoid.

Tempo

  • Drag up or down on the BPM value in the toolbar, or scroll over it.
  • Right-click it for preset tempos and half or double tempo.
  • Know the feel but not the number? Click TAP on every beat.

Turn on the metronome and play a few bars to check the speed. The Playlist grid is always 4/4. For a phrase that groups in threes, place the accents by ear on that grid.

Song key

  1. Click No key next to the BPM.
  2. Choose the root and the scale, for example C and Minor.The Piano roll shades the notes of C minor. Notes you already wrote keep their pitch.

To remove the guide, open the picker again and choose No key.

Change tempo during the song (command line)

A tempo change or ramp (for example 110 BPM speeding up to 140 into the chorus) is written with the synkopa command line, not in the app.

Needs a newer build. Tempo maps require a build with tempo-map support, available on main. Synkopa 0.9.35 uses one tempo per project: set it with the toolbar's BPM control or synkopa bpm 110.

  1. Save the project and close it in the app.
  2. Make a copy of the file and run this on the copy. It holds 110 BPM, ramps from bar 5 (beat 16), and reaches 140 BPM at bar 9 (beat 32). Beats count from 0, four to a bar. 
    synkopa -p song.synkopa apply --json '[{"op":"setTempoPoints","points":[{"beat":0,"bpm":110},{"beat":16,"bpm":110,"ramp":true},{"beat":32,"bpm":140}]}]' --label "Raise tempo into chorus"
  3. Reopen the file and play across bars 5–9.The song speeds up smoothly into bar 9.

Add --dry-run to preview the change first. For a sudden jump instead of a ramp, set "ramp":false on the point at beat 16. To remove the tempo map, apply [{"op":"setTempoPoints","points":[]}], or change the BPM in the app: that also clears the tempo points.

Line up lyrics in the Notebook

The Notebook keeps lyrics with the project, one line per bar, and highlights the current line while the song plays. That shows at a glance whether a verse fits its section.

  1. Press F8 to open the Notebook.
  2. Double-click the page tab and rename it Verse.
  3. Type one line of lyrics per bar. An empty line is a bar of rest. A heading in brackets, such as [Verse], takes no bar.
  4. Click + New page for the chorus and set its from bar to the bar of your Chorus marker.
  5. Play in SONG.The playing page's tab lights up, and on that page the current line is highlighted and scrolls along.

The text runs a bar late or early? Check from bar, and remember that every empty line counts as a bar. Lyrics are saved with the project (Ctrl + S); a deleted page comes back with Ctrl + Z.
